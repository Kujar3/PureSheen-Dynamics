PURESHEEN DYNAMICS - QUICK START
================================

PureSheen Dynamics is installed. In FL Studio:

1. Options > Manage plugins > "Find installed plugins" (or "Start scan").
2. Open the Mixer, select the MASTER track and add PureSheen Dynamics in
   the LAST effect slot. Pick the VST3 version.
3. Keep FL's Master fader at 0 dB - PureSheen works before that fader.

GET A GOOD MASTER FAST
- Click the preset name at the top and pick a starting point, for example
  "Streaming Ready" (about -14 LUFS, for Spotify / YouTube) or
  "Loud and Clear" (louder, for club and EDM tracks).
- On the Maximizer, click "Learn" while the loudest part of your song plays.
  It sets the gain so the song lands on the loudness target next to it.
- Turn on "Gain Match" (bottom right) and switch "Bypass" on and off. Gain
  Match plays both at the same loudness, so you hear whether the master
  really sounds better - not just louder.

THE CHAIN
- The strip at the top is your processing chain, left to right: EQ,
  Dynamics, Imager, Exciter, Maximizer. Drag a module to reorder it, click
  "Add" for more (a second EQ, for example), click a module to edit it.
- S solos a module, the triangle (Delta) lets you hear only what that
  module changes.
- Snapshots A-D store complete settings so you can compare versions.
- Undo / redo and the history (clock icon) are in the header.

BEFORE YOU EXPORT
- Dither (bottom right) is only needed when you export at 16 bit (CD or
  some streaming uploads). Leave it off for 24 / 32 bit files.
- Keep the Maximizer's Output at -1 dB with True Peak on for streaming.

WORKING WITH CLAUDE (OPTIONAL)
- Claude Desktop or Claude Code can read the meters and adjust the plugin.
  It stays off until you turn it on: Options (sliders button) > Claude >
  "Allow Claude to connect", then copy the setup for your Claude app from the
  same menu. Changes appear in the history, and undo reverts them.
- Claude cannot hear your music or press play, so you start playback.

The "?" button in the top-right corner of the plugin explains everything.
